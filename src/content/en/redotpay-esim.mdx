This guide is for English readers outside the United States. RedotPay does not issue a card to US residents. The eSIM pages below do not say the travel plan follows the card block lists. Checked on **8 November 2026**.

## What the eSIM is

The [eSIM product page](https://www.redotpay.com/esim), read on 8 November 2026, says you buy data with crypto before you travel and connect when you arrive, with no physical SIM and no roaming charges. It says you can stay connected in over 100 countries, pay with stablecoins, and keep your usual number active while you use the travel data. It also says travel data and everyday spending sit in one app.

The help center does not print that country count. [Getting started with eSIM](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/16106198-getting-started-with-esim) (updated 29 October 2026) says you see supported countries and regions in the app: More, then eSIM, then browse by country or region. The same page names single-country plans, regional plans such as Asia or Europe, and global plans. If the destination is missing, it says to check later because coverage may expand.

You can pay with any currency and payment method offered at checkout. What is available depends on your region and the payment provider, and it is shown before you confirm. If there is a fee, it is inside the total on the payment screen. The page does not print a separate eSIM price.

## Buy and activate

The same getting-started guide is the install path:

1. Open the RedotPay app and tap More.
2. Select eSIM.
3. Search for or select the destination.
4. Choose a plan. The page describes that choice as a data allowance and a duration.
5. Confirm that the device supports eSIM.
6. Complete payment.

The number and type of plans depend on the destination. Most locations offer several options with different durations and allowances. The page does not print those prices.

The eSIM is normally issued within a few seconds to a few minutes after payment. Status is under eSIM, then Order. After you pay, you can install it on the current device, or scan the QR code from the order details to install it on another device.

RedotPay says to install it on arrival, while the phone is on stable Wi-Fi. After it is installed and you are at the destination, turn on data roaming and set the cellular data line to the new eSIM.

Validity starts when the eSIM first connects to a local network at the destination. It does not start at purchase or at installation.

If it does not connect, the page says to check that data roaming is on and that mobile data is set to the eSIM you installed. If it still fails, the order details have steps under “Can’t connect to your eSIM?”. Past orders are under eSIM, then Order, then History.

Remaining data is under Orders, then Details, or in the phone’s own data settings.

## Top-ups

[Your guide to eSIM top-ups](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/17765699-your-guide-to-esim-top-ups) (updated 30 October 2026) says you can top up an existing eSIM if that eSIM supports top-ups. Open eSIM, then Order, select the installed eSIM, and tap Top up. The screen shows plans that are compatible with that eSIM and cover the same destination or region. Whether you can switch between a standard plan and an unlimited plan depends on the eSIM. The top-up screen is the list.

After a top-up you see the updated plan under the eSIM. You do not reinstall it and you do not scan a new QR code.

If you top up before a standard plan’s data is used up, the top-up may be queued and become active when the remaining data runs out. You can have only one queued top-up. History marks each one Active, Queued, or Used.

Top up is unavailable in two cases the page names. If an unlimited plan is still active and you see Buy more data, wait until the plan expires. If a top-up is already queued, wait until it becomes active.

Top-ups usually finish within a few minutes. Do not make another payment while one is processing. If it stays on processing, contact Support and include the order ID. The message “We're still updating your plan. Please try again in a few minutes.” means the eSIM details are still updating after issue or top-up. The page says that usually takes a few minutes.

Some eSIMs do not support top-ups. Others can be topped up only within a certain period. If top-ups are not supported, or the period has ended, you buy a new eSIM.

On a standard plan, service stops when the data is used. On an unlimited plan, after the daily high-speed allowance is used, you stay connected at a reduced speed for the rest of the day, and the high-speed allowance resets the next day. The top-up guide gives **2 GB per day** as an example of that daily high-speed allowance. It says the reduced speed may not suit streaming or large downloads. Days left are on the installed eSIM’s details page. If the eSIM supports a top-up, that is how you add data. If it does not, you buy a new plan.

The heading “Can I get a refund for a top-up?” is on that page. The paragraph under it, as published when we read it on 8 November 2026, describes the unlimited-plan allowance. It does not print a refund amount or a yes or no. The purchase rule is on the getting-started page, below.

Models marked with * may have region- or carrier-specific limits. Confirm with the manufacturer or carrier before setup.

## Limits that are easy to miss

- The plans are **data-only**. No local or roaming phone number, no traditional voice calls, and no SMS.
- They cannot receive verification codes, and they cannot register or bind WhatsApp, a bank app, or a social platform.
- You cannot pick the carrier yourself. The phone connects to the partner network. 4G or 5G depends on the plan and local coverage. The page says most locations support 4G/5G and some support 4G only.
- All eSIM purchases are final and non-refundable. The guide says the order is fulfilled with the network partners the moment you pay, the profile cannot be reversed, and cancellations or refunds are not offered.
- Validity starts at the first local connection at the destination.
- A standard plan stops when the data is gone. An unlimited plan drops to a reduced speed after the daily high-speed allowance.
- A top-up is not available on every eSIM.

Card country rules are a different set of pages. Read them before you pay an issuance fee: [supported countries](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries). Funding the app balance is in [how to top up](/en/articles/how-to-top-up-redotpay). The eSIM checkout, not that deposit table, is where the data-plan total appears.
