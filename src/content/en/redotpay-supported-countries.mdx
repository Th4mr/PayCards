These pages are written for English readers outside the United States. Before you pay an issuance fee, the practical question is whether RedotPay will let you open the app, finish KYC, and issue a card. The pages we checked do not publish a “supported countries” list in the positive. They publish block lists. This guide copies them.

On 15 November 2026, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman were not on the citizenship, location, or card-issuance lists. India was not on the citizenship or location lists, and it was on the card-issuance list, so Indian residents are ineligible for a card. Croatia, Serbia, Slovenia, and Ukraine were on the location list. A missing name is not an approval, and local law can still restrict a crypto card.

Crypto cards may be illegal where you live even when the name is missing below. Check local rules yourself. This is a reading of the company’s pages, not a legal opinion.

Checked on **15 November 2026**.

## Citizenship list read on 15 November 2026

[Unsupported Countries/Regions](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339263-unsupported-countries-regions) was marked **Updated this week** when we read it on **15 November 2026**. It names **35** places. **Ukraine** is not one of them. The page names **The Crimea region of Ukraine (including Sevastopol)** instead. Albania, Croatia, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, and Slovenia are still not among the 35. The page says a citizen of a listed place cannot complete identity verification or use the service. It does not say that a citizen of an unlisted place will be approved.

A citizen of Ukraine who is not a citizen of that Crimea region is not named on the citizenship block. That is not an approval. People **located in Ukraine** still cannot open the app, because Ukraine remains on the [location page](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14252906-location-based-access-restrictions), still dated **28 August 2026**. **Residents of Ukraine** are still ineligible for a virtual card and a physical card: Ukraine remains on the [card issuance page](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14254838-card-issuance-restrictions), which was marked **Updated this week** on the same reading. Physical cards are still not shipped to Ukraine. Ukraine remains on the [delivery page](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14255319-card-delivery-restrictions), marked **Updated yesterday** when we read it on 15 November 2026.

On that issuance reading, Albania, Kosovo, Montenegro, and North Macedonia are no longer listed. Croatia and Slovenia still are. A resident of Croatia or Slovenia is still ineligible for a virtual card and a physical card, and that place still cannot be a billing address. Getting past the citizenship page does not issue a card where the issuance list still names the place.

The location page is unchanged and still dated **28 August 2026**. Croatia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, and Slovenia remain on it. Someone located there still cannot open the app. Albania and Kosovo are not on that location page.

On the delivery reading, Albania, Kosovo, and Montenegro are no longer listed. Croatia and Slovenia still are. North Macedonia is not on the delivery list we copied. Zimbabwe is still on the citizenship list and is still not on the delivery list.

Albania and Kosovo are not on any of the four lists we read on 15 November 2026. Absence is not approval.

## How to read the lists

- **Citizenship.** The [unsupported countries article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339263-unsupported-countries-regions) (marked updated this week, read 15 November 2026) says a citizen of a listed place cannot complete KYC or use the service. The page names 35 places. The United States is on it. Ukraine is not. The Crimea region of Ukraine (including Sevastopol) is. Albania, Croatia, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, and Slovenia are not.
- **Location.** The [location article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14252906-location-based-access-restrictions) (28 August 2026) says that if you are located in a listed place, you cannot access the app. The United States is on it. Ukraine is on it. Tanzania is on it and is not on the citizenship list. This page was not redated.
- **Card issuance.** The [issuance article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14254838-card-issuance-restrictions) (marked updated this week, read 15 November 2026) says residents of the listed areas are ineligible for a virtual card and a physical card. The United States, India, Indonesia, Morocco, Nepal, Singapore, Turkey, Ukraine, Croatia, and Slovenia are on it. Albania, Kosovo, Montenegro, and North Macedonia are not.
- **Delivery.** The [delivery article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14255319-card-delivery-restrictions) (marked updated yesterday, read 15 November 2026) is about shipping plastic. Egypt, Tunisia, Palestine, and Ukraine are on that list. Egypt, Tunisia, and Palestine are not on the issuance page we checked. Do not treat that gap as a promise that a virtual card will work. Albania, Kosovo, and Montenegro are not on this delivery list.

Zimbabwe is on the citizenship list and is not on the delivery list we copied. North Macedonia is on the location list only. Montenegro is on the location list only. Croatia and Slovenia are on location, issuance, and delivery, and not on the citizenship list. Use each page for the rule it states.

Searches for Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Algeria are common. None of those three names is on the four lists we re-read on 15 November 2026. Absence is not approval, and a name on a list is a block. [RedotPay in Pakistan](/en/articles/redotpay-pakistan) reads those lists next to the Send and P2P pages. It does not say the card works there. Saudi Arabia is absent from the same four lists. The Arabic page puts that absence next to the Saudi Capital Market Authority warnings: [RedotPay in Saudi Arabia](/articles/redotpay-saudi-arabia). This English page is not an Algeria signup guide. Egypt is on the delivery list and not on the issuance list we checked. Iraq is on the citizenship, location, issuance, and delivery lists. When an address is allowed to receive plastic, timing is in the [delivery guide](/en/articles/redotpay-physical-card-delivery). Travel data is a separate product: [RedotPay eSIM](/en/articles/redotpay-esim). Borrowing against crypto is a separate product from country eligibility: [RedotPay Credit](/en/articles/redotpay-credit).

## If you need the card for cash

ATM withdrawal needs the physical card. If the address is on the delivery list, do not pay 100 USD in order to withdraw cash. The virtual card does not withdraw. Fees for cards that do ship are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal).

## Before you tap signup

1. Find your citizenship on the verification list and your shipping address on the delivery list.
2. Stop if the invite page or the app rejects the document country or the address.
3. Do not pay 10 USD (virtual) or 100 USD (physical) until the app shows the order as available. The [fee table](/en/articles/redotpay-fees) has the sources.
4. Read the law where you live. Company acceptance is not local permission.

Account steps, after that check, are in the [referral code guide](/en/articles/redotpay-referral-code).

## What “supported” should mean in a review

A review that says RedotPay “works worldwide” or “works in the US” is ahead of the company’s own pages. Those pages say US residents cannot be issued a card, people located in the United States cannot open the app, and US citizens cannot finish KYC. Leaving the citizenship list is not the same as leaving the issuance list. Ukraine shows that split: the country name left the citizenship page, and residents of Ukraine are still on the issuance page. If RedotPay edits an article, the stamp on their page will move, and we will move the date on this one after we re-read it.
