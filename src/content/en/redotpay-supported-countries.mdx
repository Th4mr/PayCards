Before you pay an issuance fee, the practical question is whether RedotPay will verify you and, if you need plastic, whether it will ship. The pages we checked do not publish a “supported countries” list in the positive. They publish two block lists. This guide copies them and says what they do not mean.

Crypto cards may be illegal where you live even when the name is missing below. Check local rules yourself. This is a reading of the company’s pages, not a legal opinion.

Checked on **24 October 2026**.

## How to read the lists

- The **verification list** is about **citizenship**. The [unsupported countries article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339263-unsupported-countries-regions) (28 August 2026) says a citizen of a listed place cannot complete identity verification or use the service. **United States** is on it. So are Mainland China, Russia, Iran, North Korea, and the other names in the grid above.
- The **delivery list** is wider. The [card delivery restrictions](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14255319-card-delivery-restrictions) article (21 July 2026) blocks physical shipping. Places that are **not** on the citizenship block but still do not receive plastic include Egypt, India, Indonesia, Morocco, Nepal, Palestine, Tunisia, and Turkey.
- North Macedonia and Zimbabwe are on the citizenship block and are not on the delivery list we copied. The delivery list is not a perfect superset. Use both pages.
- Gulf countries that were **not** on the citizenship block when we checked — Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman — plus Jordan and Algeria, are absent from that block only. Absence is not a statement that the account will open or that the card is legal there.

Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, and Palestine show the gap clearly: not on the published KYC block, and on the shipping block. Do not treat that as a promise about the virtual card.

## If you need the card for cash

ATM withdrawal needs the physical card. If the address is on the delivery list, do not pay 100 USD in order to withdraw cash. The virtual card does not withdraw. Fees for cards that do ship are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal).

## Before you tap signup

1. Find your citizenship on the verification list and your shipping address on the delivery list.
2. Stop if the invite page or the app rejects the document country or the address.
3. Do not pay 10 USD (virtual) or 100 USD (physical) until the app shows the order as available. The [fee table](/en/articles/redotpay-fees) has the sources.
4. Read the law where you live. Company acceptance is not local permission.

Account steps, after that check, are in the [referral code guide](/en/articles/redotpay-referral-code).

## What “supported” should mean in a review

A review that says RedotPay “works worldwide” or “works in the US” is ahead of the company’s own page. The page we read says the opposite for US citizenship. If RedotPay edits the article, the date on their page will move, and we will move the date on this one after we re-read it.
