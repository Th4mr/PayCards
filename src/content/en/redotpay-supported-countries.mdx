These pages are written for English readers outside the United States. Before you pay an issuance fee, the practical question is whether RedotPay will let you open the app, finish KYC, and issue a card. The pages we checked do not publish a “supported countries” list in the positive. They publish block lists. This guide copies them.

On 8 November 2026, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman were not on the citizenship, location, or card-issuance lists. India was not on the citizenship or location lists, and it was on the card-issuance list, so Indian residents are ineligible for a card. Croatia, Serbia, Slovenia, and Ukraine were on the location list. A missing name is not an approval, and local law can still restrict a crypto card.

Crypto cards may be illegal where you live even when the name is missing below. Check local rules yourself. This is a reading of the company’s pages, not a legal opinion.

Checked on **8 November 2026**.

## Citizenship list on 2 November 2026

[Unsupported Countries/Regions](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339263-unsupported-countries-regions) was updated on **2 November 2026** (the page timestamp is 2 November 2026). We re-read it on 8 November 2026. It names **35** places. Albania, Croatia, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, and Slovenia are not among them. The page says a citizen of a listed place cannot complete identity verification or use the service. It does not say that a citizen of an unlisted place will be approved.

A citizen of those six countries is no longer named on that block, so the citizenship page no longer says they cannot finish KYC. They may get through identity checks only if the app accepts the document. That is not an approval.

All six are still on the [card issuance page](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14254838-card-issuance-restrictions), which is still dated **28 August 2026**. A resident of any of them is still ineligible for a virtual card and a physical card, and that place still cannot be a billing address. Getting past the citizenship page does not issue a card to a resident of a place that remains on the issuance list.

Four of the six remain on the [location page](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14252906-location-based-access-restrictions), also still dated **28 August 2026**: Croatia, Montenegro, North Macedonia, and Slovenia. Someone located there still cannot open the app. Albania and Kosovo are not on that location page.

Five of the six remain on the [delivery page](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14255319-card-delivery-restrictions), still dated **21 July 2026**: Albania, Croatia, Kosovo, Montenegro, and Slovenia. North Macedonia is not on the delivery list we copied. Zimbabwe is still on the citizenship list and is still not on the delivery list.

We compared the location, issuance, and delivery names on 8 November 2026 with the copies already on this site. Those three pages were not redated, and the names match.

## How to read the lists

- **Citizenship.** The [unsupported countries article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339263-unsupported-countries-regions) (2 November 2026) says a citizen of a listed place cannot complete KYC or use the service. The page names 35 places. The United States is on it. Albania, Croatia, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia, and Slovenia are not.
- **Location.** The [location article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14252906-location-based-access-restrictions) (28 August 2026) says that if you are located in a listed place, you cannot access the app. The United States is on it. Tanzania is on it and is not on the citizenship list.
- **Card issuance.** The [issuance article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14254838-card-issuance-restrictions) (28 August 2026) says residents of the listed areas are ineligible for a virtual card and a physical card. The United States, India, Indonesia, Morocco, Nepal, Singapore, and Turkey are on it.
- **Delivery.** The [delivery article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14255319-card-delivery-restrictions) (21 July 2026) is about shipping plastic. Egypt, Tunisia, and Palestine are on that list and are not on the issuance page we checked. Do not treat that gap as a promise that a virtual card will work.

Zimbabwe is on the citizenship list and is not on the delivery list we copied. North Macedonia left the citizenship list on 2 November 2026 and was already absent from that delivery list. It is still on the location list and the issuance list. Use each page for the rule it states.

Searches for Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Algeria are common. None of those three names is on the four lists we re-read on 8 November 2026. Absence is not approval, and a name on a list is a block. [RedotPay in Pakistan](/en/articles/redotpay-pakistan) reads those lists next to the Send and P2P pages. It does not say the card works there. Saudi Arabia is absent from the same four lists. The Arabic page puts that absence next to the Saudi Capital Market Authority warnings: [RedotPay in Saudi Arabia](/articles/redotpay-saudi-arabia). This English page is not an Algeria signup guide. Egypt is on the delivery list and not on the issuance list we checked. Iraq is on the citizenship, location, issuance, and delivery lists. When an address is allowed to receive plastic, timing is in the [delivery guide](/en/articles/redotpay-physical-card-delivery). Travel data is a separate product: [RedotPay eSIM](/en/articles/redotpay-esim).

## If you need the card for cash

ATM withdrawal needs the physical card. If the address is on the delivery list, do not pay 100 USD in order to withdraw cash. The virtual card does not withdraw. Fees for cards that do ship are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal).

## Before you tap signup

1. Find your citizenship on the verification list and your shipping address on the delivery list.
2. Stop if the invite page or the app rejects the document country or the address.
3. Do not pay 10 USD (virtual) or 100 USD (physical) until the app shows the order as available. The [fee table](/en/articles/redotpay-fees) has the sources.
4. Read the law where you live. Company acceptance is not local permission.

Account steps, after that check, are in the [referral code guide](/en/articles/redotpay-referral-code).

## What “supported” should mean in a review

A review that says RedotPay “works worldwide” or “works in the US” is ahead of the company’s own pages. Those pages say US residents cannot be issued a card, people located in the United States cannot open the app, and US citizens cannot finish KYC. Leaving the citizenship list is not the same as leaving the issuance list. If RedotPay edits an article, the date on their page will move, and we will move the date on this one after we re-read it.
