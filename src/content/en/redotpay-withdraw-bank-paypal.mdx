“RedotPay withdraw” covers four different products. Mixing them is how people expect a bank transfer and get an on-chain address, or expect PayPal to behave like a salary account. Checked on **24 October 2026**. The pages below are the ones we read. They do not publish one withdrawal fee for every path.

## EUR or GBP to a bank

[Can I withdraw EUR or GBP to a bank account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11991671-can-i-withdraw-eur-or-gbp-to-a-bank-account) (30 July 2026) says yes. Select the EUR or GBP balance and withdraw to a **linked bank account in the same currency**.

The page does not mention USD, and it does not print a RedotPay fee. [Who processes my transfer, and are there any additional bank fees?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/17693207-who-processes-my-transfer-and-are-there-any-additional-bank-fees) (21 October 2026) says a partner bank or payment provider processes the transfer, depending on currency, destination, and method. An intermediary or the recipient’s bank may deduct fees. The recipient can receive less than you sent.

That is not an IBAN product page. If the app does not show a linked bank for the currency you hold, this article is not a workaround.

## USDs stay inside the app

[Can I withdraw USDs?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10693389-can-i-withdraw-usds) (28 July 2026): **no.** USDs are digital tokens used inside the app. They cannot be withdrawn to an external wallet or a bank account.

- **General balance:** does not expire. Use it for card purchases, card fees, or sending to other RedotPay users.
- **Reward balance:** purchases only, and it usually expires after 30 days. The 5 USD sign-up reward is this kind of balance. It does not pay issuance. Detail is on the [fees page](/en/articles/redotpay-fees).

## Crypto, on-chain

[How do I withdraw crypto from RedotPay to another wallet or platform?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339292-how-do-i-withdraw-crypto-from-redotpay-to-another-wallet-or-platform) (28 July 2026):

1. Open the app and tap More.
2. Tap Withdraw.
3. Select Withdraw on-chain.
4. Choose the crypto and the network. The example on the page is **USDT** and **USDT-TRC20**. The balance must be above the minimum withdrawal amount. The page does not print that minimum.
5. Copy the deposit address from the destination wallet for **that same crypto and network**.
6. Paste it, tap Withdraw, and finish verification.

A wrong network is not described as recoverable. Deposit networks are chosen on the Deposit screen when money comes **in**. That direction is the [top-up guide](/en/articles/how-to-top-up-redotpay). Do not reuse an old screenshot for either direction.

## The card is not a transfer button

[Does the RedotPay card support cash transfers?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10621387-does-the-redotpay-card-support-cash-transfers) (27 July 2026): **no.** You cannot send funds through transfer services or digital wallets including **bKash, Revolut, Skrill, URPAY, and Wise.**

ATM cash is a different action, physical card only. Fees and the chart’s ATM caps are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal) and the [limits guide](/en/articles/redotpay-limits).

## PayPal

[Third-party transfer support (e.g. PayPal)](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11331113-third-party-transfer-support-e-g-paypal-fee-discount-in-may) (21 July 2026) says RedotPay can **receive** a transfer from a platform such as PayPal:

- Start the transfer on that platform and select the **RedotPay card** as the recipient.
- Funds are converted to **USDT** and credited to the RedotPay balance.
- Pending in the app means the funds arrived and the credit is processing.
- **Standard fee: 3%.** If the calculated fee is lower, a **minimum of 1 USD** applies.
- The same page describes a **May 2025** offer: 1% and no minimum, from 1 May 2025 00:00 to 31 May 2025 23:59 UTC. That window is over. We are not quoting 1% as today’s fee.
- Availability is “selected countries and regions” and can depend on the other platform. The page says to try it. A successful transfer is the confirmation, not this article.

Linking the card **to** PayPal so you can pay is the other feature. [The payments article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11027588-how-do-i-use-my-redotpay-card-for-payments) (21 July 2026) says the virtual card can be linked to Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal. A new card may need **up to 5 days** to sync, and most are ready within 2. See the [Apple Pay guide](/en/articles/redotpay-apple-pay-google-pay).

Neither page says RedotPay replaces PayPal for a freelancer who needs to invoice a client. We did not find a published comparison of those fees. If a client can pay your RedotPay card from PayPal, the receive article above is the one that states the 3% line. If they cannot, the article says availability varies.

## P2P

[What is RedotPay P2P?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041647-what-is-redotpay-p2p) (24 July 2026): a marketplace in the app where you buy and sell digital currencies with other users, in local currencies you choose, at prices you set, with more than one payment method.

[How does RedotPay P2P work?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041650-how-does-redotpay-p2p-work) (5 August 2026): ads are posted by verified advertisers, the marketplace supports **over 200 payment options**, and you follow the ad, then release or receive funds and rate the trade.

[Who can trade on RedotPay P2P?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041698-who-can-trade-on-redotpay-p2p) (24 July 2026): anyone who has completed identity verification. Posting your own ads requires advertiser status. **Availability may vary by region.**

We did not find a P2P commission on those three pages. Do not copy a percentage from a forum into this step. P2P is also not a way around a country block. The [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) still applies to the account.

<CTABox lang="en" />

Cash from an ATM, a bank withdrawal in EUR or GBP, an on-chain USDT transfer, a PayPal receive, and a P2P trade are five exits. Pick the one the app is actually offering for the balance you hold.
