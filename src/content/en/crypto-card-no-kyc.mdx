“Crypto card no KYC” is a high-volume search, including “Apple Pay”, “Google Pay”, “virtual”, and “prepaid” on the end of it. The honest answer for RedotPay is short.

**RedotPay requires KYC.** A card number is not issued on the pages we read until identity verification is done. This guide will not explain how to dodge that check, which document to fake, or which other brand claims to skip it.

Checked on **24 October 2026**.

## What RedotPay actually asks for

[Understanding identity verification](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339264-understanding-identity-verification) (26 July 2026) says verification is required. It names **Sumsub** as the provider. It lists your name, date of birth, and a passport, national ID, or driver’s licence, plus the extra checks the app shows for your case.

The same pages do not publish one review time for every country. Anyone selling a “fast approval” is not RedotPay. Do not pay them, and do not send them photos of your documents.

The [referral guide](/en/articles/redotpay-referral-code) is where the account steps live. A referral code does not remove KYC. The 5 USD sign-up reward cannot pay the card fee either.

## Who cannot finish the check

Three different limits show up in the help center, and they are not the same sentence:

- US residents are ineligible for a virtual or physical card.
- People located in the United States cannot access the app.
- US citizens cannot complete KYC.

Other countries are named on citizenship, location, issuance, or delivery lists. India is one of the names on the issuance list in our [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries). A country that does not appear is **not** an approval. Read the lists before you upload a passport.

If the check is rejected, the useful move is the one the app describes: a clearer photo, or the document it asked for. We do not have a measured pass rate.

## What people hope a no-KYC card will do

Search suggestions add Apple Pay and Google Pay. On RedotPay, wallet linking is a feature of the **virtual card after** verification. The [payments article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11027588-how-do-i-use-my-redotpay-card-for-payments) says the virtual card can be linked to Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal. The wallet in your country can still refuse it. Skipping KYC is not how that link is created. See the [Apple Pay guide](/en/articles/redotpay-apple-pay-google-pay).

The same hope shows up as “virtual crypto card no KYC” and “prepaid card no verification”. RedotPay’s virtual card is 10 USD once, and it still requires the identity check. The [virtual card guide](/en/articles/virtual-card-online-payments) and the [prepaid Visa guide](/en/articles/prepaid-visa-card-online) keep those products separate from the no-check ads.

## If you will not complete KYC

Then do not open this account in order to force it through. A different company may publish different rules. Read that company’s KYC page yourself. We are not listing a “no KYC” winner, and we are not pasting fees from a forum.

[Is RedotPay safe?](/en/articles/is-redotpay-safe) covers what the help center says about screening and freezes. Know Your Transaction (4 September 2026) says RedotPay may hold, freeze, or decline a transaction. Identity checks are part of that design, not an optional extra.

If the app will verify you, the card is a normal RedotPay account after that. If it will not, this is the wrong product, and no referral link changes the list.
