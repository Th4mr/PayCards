Search suggestions pair RedotPay with Bybit more often than with the other card names. That does not make the two products the same shape, and it does not give us Bybit’s prices. Checked on **24 October 2026**. Bybit’s fees stay on Bybit’s site.

## Two different cards

**RedotPay** is a spending app. You deposit crypto, then the card spends it. You do not need a Bybit account. The [review](/en/articles/redotpay-review) quotes what the card is, including the Visa programmes named for Hong Kong and Singapore and the later “major card networks” wording.

**Bybit Card** is an exchange card. It spends a balance that already sits on Bybit. You skip a separate deposit and a network fee. You also depend on that exchange for both trading and spending. If Bybit restricts the country, the card stops with it. We are not stating which countries Bybit allows. Open Bybit’s country page.

Crypto.com’s card is the same kind of product as Bybit Card for this comparison: an exchange balance. Wirex is usually described as a multi-currency app. Binance Card is another exchange card people search next to RedotPay. Their prices are not in this article. The wider notes are on [RedotPay vs other crypto cards](/en/articles/redotpay-vs-other-crypto-cards).

## What to compare, in order

1. **Can the account open, and can the card be issued?** RedotPay’s lists are in the [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries). US citizens cannot finish KYC. People located in the United States cannot open the app. US residents cannot be issued a virtual or a physical card. A lower fee on a card you cannot get is not a saving. Do the same check on Bybit’s site. We did not copy Bybit’s country list.
2. **What you pay RedotPay for the way you spend.** The numbers we checked are below. Bybit’s column is a link you open yourself, not a figure.
3. **How the money arrives.** A crypto deposit into RedotPay has a network fee charged by the network, not by RedotPay. An exchange card spends the balance already there.
4. **Cash.** RedotPay ATM cash needs the physical card. Caps and the 2% / 3% fee are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal) and the [limits guide](/en/articles/redotpay-limits). We are not stating Bybit’s ATM rules.
5. **Wallets.** RedotPay’s payments page says the virtual card can be linked to Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal. A wallet can still refuse it. See the [Apple Pay guide](/en/articles/redotpay-apple-pay-google-pay).
