People search “best crypto card”, “best crypto cards 2026”, and the same phrase with a country on the end. Those lists usually fill every column with fees from other blogs. This page will not. RedotPay is one option we can describe from its own help center. Everyone else stays a link.

Checked on **24 October 2026**. If the app disagrees, the app wins.

## What “best” actually depends on

1. **Whether the account opens.** A lower fee on a card you cannot get is not a saving. US residents are ineligible for a RedotPay card. Other countries are named on RedotPay’s block lists. A country that is missing is not an approval. Read the [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) before you pay an issuance fee.
2. **How you already hold the crypto.** If the coins sit in a separate wallet, a spending app means a deposit and a network fee. If they already sit on an exchange that will issue you a card, you may skip that transfer. That is a product difference, not a ranking.
3. **The fee for the payment you make.** Issuance, a currency that is not the card currency, and ATM cash are different prices. A card that looks cheap online can be expensive at a cash machine.
4. **KYC.** RedotPay requires identity verification. A search for a card with no KYC is a different question, answered in [crypto card no KYC](/en/articles/crypto-card-no-kyc).
5. **The merchant.** Help pages can say a network is widely accepted and still say some merchants block prepaid or crypto-linked cards. We never claim a named shop will approve the payment.

## A short way to decide

- You want a card that is not your trading account, you can pass RedotPay’s KYC, and you have priced 10 USD or 100 USD plus the conversion that matches your payments. RedotPay is a candidate. The named comparison, still without invented competitor prices, is [RedotPay vs other crypto cards](/en/articles/redotpay-vs-other-crypto-cards).
- Your balance already sits on an exchange. Read that exchange’s card page before you move the coins.
- You need cash. The virtual card does not withdraw. ATM cash needs the physical card and is usually the expensive path. See [how to convert USDT to cash](/en/articles/convert-usdt-to-cash).
- You only pay online. Start with the [virtual card guide](/en/articles/virtual-card-online-payments) and do not order plastic until you know the address can receive it.
