“RedotPay P2P”, “RedotPay P2P fees”, and “RedotPay P2P safe” are searches for a marketplace, not for an ATM. Checked on **15 November 2026**.

## What the marketplace is

[What is RedotPay P2P?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041647-what-is-redotpay-p2p) (24 July 2026) says it is a marketplace inside the app: you buy and sell with other users, in local currencies, at prices you set, with more than one payment method. 
[How does RedotPay P2P work?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041650-how-does-redotpay-p2p-work) (5 August 2026) adds three points: ads are posted by verified advertisers who passed checks, the marketplace supports **over 200 payment options**, and you follow the ad, release or receive funds when both sides have done their part, then rate the trade.

[Who can trade on RedotPay P2P?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041698-who-can-trade-on-redotpay-p2p) (24 July 2026) says anyone who has completed identity verification can trade. Posting your own ads requires **advertiser** status. Availability may vary by region. A merchant, in these pages, is an advertiser, not a RedotPay employee.

This is not a way around a country block. The [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) still applies. Cash is a different product: [convert USDT to cash](/en/articles/convert-usdt-to-cash). EUR or GBP to a bank is in the [withdrawal guide](/en/articles/redotpay-withdraw-bank-paypal).

## Fees: 0 on RedotPay’s button, not 0 in total

[How to buy stablecoins via RedotPay P2P](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041775-how-to-buy-stablecoins-via-redotpay-p2p) (5 August 2026) tells you to tap **Buy with 0 fees**. [How to sell](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041815-how-to-sell-stablecoins-via-redotpay-p2p) (5 August 2026) tells you to tap **Sell with 0 fees**. That phrase is RedotPay’s fee on the order, not the price of the coin and not your bank’s charge.

[RedotPay P2P Trading Code of Conduct](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/13250558-redotpay-p2p-trading-code-of-conduct) (5 August 2026) says that unless you agree otherwise in the P2P chat first, **you cover the sending fees and the seller covers the receiving fees**. Collecting extra fees without a contract or platform approval is a violation, with a penalty from a warning to a 7–60 day ban or a permanent ban depending on severity.

The counterparty sets the rate in the ad. A rate that looks cheap and then changes during the trade is listed as false advertising. Read the ad’s price and terms before you place the order. The dispute page repeats that tip.

## Buy USDT

The buy page, using USDT as its example:

1. Open the app, tap **More**, then **P2P**.
2. Choose a seller. You can filter by amount or payment method, then tap **Buy**.
3. Enter the amount of USDT.
4. Read the seller’s terms.
5. Tap **Buy with 0 fees**. The platform locks the seller’s matching crypto.
6. Tap **Get payment details** and pay the seller’s registered account for that method.
7. Tap **Transferred, notify seller**. Status becomes Awaiting Confirmation.
8. Wait. When the seller confirms, the crypto is released to your balance.

To cancel before completion: **Cancel order**, pick a reason, **Submit**. Tapping “I have paid” before you have paid is malicious order blocking under the code of conduct and can restrict the account.

## Sell USDT

The sell page:

1. More, then P2P.
2. Tap **Sell**, choose the crypto, and read buyers’ methods, time limits, and conditions.
3. Enter the local-currency amount you want, or the crypto quantity.
4. Tap **Sell with 0 fees**.
5. Wait for the buyer to pay. Chat stays inside the order if you need it.
6. When you are notified, confirm the money is in your bank or wallet, and that the payer matches the buyer’s details.
7. Tap **Confirm payment**, then **Confirm**, and complete **2FA** to release.

The page’s own warning: do not release cryptocurrency before you have confirmed receipt.

## How not to get cheated

[How to avoid scams on RedotPay P2P](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12042044-how-to-avoid-scams-on-redotpay-p2p) (5 August 2026) says the product has safeguards and that users still have to watch for fraud. The patterns it names:

| Scam | What the page says to do |
|---|---|
| Fake payment proof | Check the bank or wallet before you release. Do not rely on a screenshot or a verbal confirmation. |
| Fake SMS | Confirm in the bank or wallet app. SMS can be faked. |
| Refund after a cheque or a third-party payment | Match the payer’s name to the name verified on RedotPay. Do not take third-party accounts. A cheque can be reversed. Do not release if you suspect fraud. Dispute at once. |
| Man in the middle | Someone on Telegram or WhatsApp pretends to be a merchant, sends a fake ad link, or steers payment to a stranger. Stay inside RedotPay P2P. Third-party payments are not supported. |

Do not put words such as BTC, cryptocurrency, or “buy crypto” in the payment note. The code of conduct lists that as a buyer rule because those words can trigger restrictions on the receiving account.

## If the trade breaks

[RedotPay P2P Dispute Guide](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12042111-redotpay-p2p-dispute-guide) says: on the order, tap **Need help**. You can contact the other party, read suggested fixes, or **Initiate Dispute**, describe the problem, upload images, video, or files, and submit. You see “Dispute in progress”. The team responds within **24 hours**. Updates go to your registered email. If you settle it, **Cancel dispute** returns the order to its previous status.

[Dispute handling guidelines](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12042120-redotpay-p2p-dispute-handling-guidelines) (4 September 2026) are more specific, and they limit what RedotPay will do:

- RedotPay is **not liable** for P2P losses and **cannot guarantee** recovery. Outcome depends on evidence and on whether the other person cooperates.
- Instant payment, seller silent: a specialist asks for release. If there is no timely response, the order can be completed after your **video proof** is verified.
- Non-instant payment: if the seller proves they did not receive the money after **6 days**, the order is canceled. If you have valid video proof and the seller cannot prove non-payment, payment is assumed and the order is completed.
- You paid more than the order: the specialist asks the seller to refund the difference. If the seller refuses, **you bear the loss**.
- Seller gave no payment method, pushed you off-platform, or used a third-party account: treated as a violation and possible fraud. If you have not paid, the order is canceled after checks. If you have paid, valid evidence can force a refund or completion.
- Name mismatch or third-party payment: RedotPay is not responsible for the loss and does not guarantee a refund. Trading can be suspended. Underpayment must be topped up, with extra fees on the buyer. If you already released on an underpayment and the buyer refuses the rest, **you bear the loss**.
- You released and never got paid: you are responsible for confirming payment first. RedotPay is not liable and cannot guarantee recovery. You can report it, and you can go to law enforcement. RedotPay will not share personal data without official legal documents, and it says it will cooperate if authorities contact them.
- Confirmed fraud: P2P trading is permanently disabled, ads are delisted, and funds may be frozen for **60 days**. If the offender’s balance is too low, recovery is not guaranteed.

The code of conduct applies from registration and covers users and advertisers. Fraud can mean a permanent P2P ban and a 60-day freeze. Stay reachable while an order or a dispute is open.

## Becoming an advertiser

[How to become a P2P advertiser](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041974-how-to-become-a-p2p-advertiser) (5 August 2026) starts from the P2P profile or from Post Ad, then Apply. The first currency follows the region on your identity check. You can add others, including USD and EUR, up to three currencies. The page asks for identity, source of funds, crypto liquidity, P2P activity, and advertiser status elsewhere. Up to three applications a month. Three rejections in a row mean a 30-day wait. [Requirements for posting ads](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041986-requirements-for-posting-ads-on-redotpay-p2p) (5 August 2026) say only advertiser status can post.

## A short checklist

1. Finish KYC in the app before you expect the P2P menu.
2. Compare the ad price with what you would get by depositing crypto and spending on the card. The card path is in the [top-up guide](/en/articles/how-to-top-up-redotpay). Neither path prints one universal rate.
3. Pay only to the account shown inside the order, from an account in your verified name, and only after you have read the time limit.
4. Release only after the bank or wallet shows the money, then complete 2FA.
5. If it stalls, dispute inside the order within the flow above. Do not move the chat to another app to “finish faster”.
