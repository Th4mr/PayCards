“Convert USDT to cash” sounds like one button. It is three different outcomes, and only one of them is notes in your hand.

Checked on **24 October 2026**. Figures below are RedotPay’s. An exchange or a bank has its own table. Open that table. We are not copying it.

## Cash, a bank balance, and crypto that only feels like cash

| Outcome | What you actually receive | On RedotPay |
|---|---|---|
| Notes | ATM cash | Physical card only. The virtual card cannot withdraw. |
| Bank balance | Fiat in a bank account | EUR or GBP to a linked account in the same currency. USDs cannot go to a bank. |
| Still crypto | USDT in another wallet | An on-chain withdrawal. The deposit and withdraw articles use networks the app shows. This is not cash. |
| Spending, not cash | A card payment in a shop | Possible where the network is accepted. See [how to spend USDT](/en/articles/how-to-spend-usdt). |

Search suggestions add a city, Coinbase, Binance, Trust Wallet, or a local wallet such as Vodafone Cash. RedotPay’s card [cannot cash-transfer](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10621387-which-methods-or-apps-are-not-supported-for-cash-transfers) to bKash, Revolut, Skrill, URPAY, or Wise (27 July 2026). We did not find Vodafone Cash, Sham Cash, or Zain Cash on the pages we read. Those are not a hidden RedotPay menu.

## The ATM path

[Can I withdraw cash from an ATM, and are there any fees?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10622095-can-i-withdraw-cash-from-an-atm-and-are-there-any-fees) (27 July 2026) is about the **physical** card, at ATMs that support major card networks.

- USD card: **2%** up to 10,000 USD in a month, then **3%** above that.
- HKD card: **2%**.
- A transaction in another currency: **1.2%** on that page.
- Crypto conversion: **1%**. The page does not say this line is only for ATM withdrawals.
- The ATM operator’s fee is separate. RedotPay does not waive it.
- Annual Pro waives RedotPay’s own ATM fees on up to **1,000 USD** a month. The monthly plan does not. The operator’s fee remains.

The limitations chart, on a page updated 24 July 2026, shows ATM caps of 3 withdrawals a day and 30 a month, a daily cap of 3,750 USD or 23,000 HKD, and a monthly cap of 50,000 USD or 75,000 HKD. A footnote says some USD cards are 3,000 USD a day and 10,000 USD a month. The app can show a lower limit. The full table is in the [limits guide](/en/articles/redotpay-limits) and the fee lines are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal).

Ordering the plastic is **100 USD** once. Production is usually 10–15 business days, then delivery. Standard mail is marked waived and DHL is calculated at checkout. If the address cannot receive the card, do not pay the 100 USD for an ATM plan. See [delivery](/en/articles/redotpay-physical-card-delivery).

## The bank path, which is not “USDT to my bank”

[Withdrawing USDs](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10693389-withdrawing-usds) (28 July 2026) says USDs cannot be withdrawn to an external wallet or a bank. [EUR and GBP bank withdrawals](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11991671-eur-gbp-bank-withdrawals) (30 July 2026) describe a payout to a linked account in that same currency. Partner bank fees are a separate page. The [bank and PayPal guide](/en/articles/redotpay-withdraw-bank-paypal) keeps those limits in one place.

An on-chain withdrawal of USDT is crypto moving to a wallet. It can be the right step if you will sell somewhere else. It is not cash.

P2P inside RedotPay is a user marketplace. [Who can trade](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12041698-who-can-trade-on-redotpay-p2p) (24 July 2026) says anyone who finished identity verification can trade, posting ads needs advertiser status, and availability may vary by region. We did not find a P2P fee. It is not a cash desk.

## Before you chase a rate

You still need an account RedotPay will open, and identity verification. US residents are ineligible for a card. A country missing from the lists is not an approval. See the [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) and [crypto card no KYC](/en/articles/crypto-card-no-kyc).

Selling USDT on an exchange you already use can be simpler when the card cannot be delivered. That exchange’s fee page is the price. We are not reprinting Coinbase, Binance, or Kraken rates.
