“RedotPay declined” is a cluster of different help articles, not one fee. This page quotes those articles. Figures were checked on **24 October 2026**. If the app shows another amount, trust the app.

## What to check at checkout

[What should I do if my payment fails on the checkout page?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10621406-what-should-i-do-if-my-payment-fails-on-the-checkout-page) (23 July 2026) asks you to check three things:

1. **Est. Total Value.** It is approximate, and it includes a fee. Matching the item price is not enough.
2. **Payment currency**, under Profile → Payment Settings → Payment priority.
3. **A limit you set yourself**, under Card → Limit. That cap is yours. The published card caps are on the [limits guide](/en/articles/redotpay-limits).

If a different merchant accepts the same card, that article says the problem is the merchant, not a dead card.

[How can I avoid declined card payments?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10834021-how-can-i-avoid-declined-card-payments) (updated 8 October 2026) adds the balance rule: the balance must cover the payment **plus** network and processing fees. The example on the page is that a balance of exactly 100 USD may not be enough. Turn on low-balance alerts. Saved merchants — the page names Netflix, Spotify, iCloud, cloud storage, and AI tools — keep charging. If the card is frozen, lost, deleted, or expired, or the balance is short, the charge declines and a fee may apply. Cancel the subscription on the merchant’s site. RedotPay cannot cancel it for you. The separate [cancel article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10622376-how-do-i-cancel-a-subscription-with-a-merchant) (23 July 2026) says the same thing.

The card has to be **active**. Enable the crypto asset, the merchant’s currency, and the transaction type you are using: online, in-store, or ATM. Do not exceed the single-transaction or daily limit. Some merchants and some transaction types are unsupported. The page suggests a small test, and it says a small decline fee may apply. It does not print the amount. The amount is on the chart below.

## Declined, pending, and the 0.5 USD line

These sentences come from different pages. They are not interchangeable.

| What the page says | Source |
|---|---|
| Declined means rejected. No funds are deducted. A temporary authorized amount is a pre-auth hold and clears on its own. A small decline fee may still apply. The page does not state the amount. | [What does Declined mean?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11130237-what-does-declined-mean-on-my-transaction) (27 July 2026) |
| If the transaction was truly declined, you are not charged. A pending hold is usually refunded within 2 to 5 days. | [Why was I charged for a declined transaction?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10621399-why-was-i-charged-for-a-declined-transaction) (24 July 2026) |
| First 3 declined transactions per card each month: waived. Each following decline: 0.5 USD. The chart lists insufficient balance, frozen cards, lost cards, and incorrect card information. That list is not exhaustive. | Chart image on [RedotPay Card Limitations & Fees](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339271-redotpay-card-limitations-fees) (page updated 24 July 2026). We read the image on 24 October 2026. |

The chart is an image, not a sentence in the article body. We are quoting what that image shows. A later image would replace these numbers. The same chart also shows **0.2 USD** after the first 5 small authorizations per card each month, for transactions of 1.00 USD or less. That is a small-authorization line, not the decline line.

[Why was my payment declined with no record in the app?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10654983-why-was-my-payment-declined-with-no-record-in-the-app) (27 July 2026) is a fourth case: the merchant stopped the payment before RedotPay received it. There is nothing to dispute inside RedotPay. Contact the merchant. An active card should work elsewhere.

## When the merchant is the block

[Why won’t a merchant accept my RedotPay card?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/12410141-why-won-t-a-merchant-accept-my-redotpay-card) (15 July 2026) gives three reasons: the merchant does not accept the card’s network, their system blocks the payment for security, or RedotPay restricts it to protect the account. The tries it lists are: confirm the network, pay through Apple Pay or Google Pay instead, or ask support whether the account is restricted.

Named cases live on their own pages. AliExpress, Google Play error OR-HDT-16, game credits, and the customer-feedback list that includes ChatGPT and Netflix are in the [merchant guide](/en/articles/redotpay-netflix-chatgpt). Do not treat a decline there as proof the card is broken everywhere.

A new card may also fail to **link**. [Why won’t my RedotPay card link to PayPal, Google Pay, or other apps?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10621389-why-won-t-my-redotpay-card-link-to-paypal-google-pay-or-other-apps) (24 July 2026) says syncing takes time. Most cards are ready within 2 days, and it can take up to 5. Retrying too often can freeze the card. The name and the billing address have to match. Linking steps for the wallets are in the [Apple Pay and Google Pay guide](/en/articles/redotpay-apple-pay-google-pay).

## A short order of checks

1. Read the estimated total and the fee, not only the price.
2. Confirm the payment currency and any limit under Card → Limit.
3. Confirm the card is active and the asset, currency, and transaction type are enabled.
4. If only one merchant fails, stop and read that merchant’s help page.
5. If the app shows Declined, do not assume the purchase was taken. Read the pending line, and remember the chart’s 0.5 USD line is separate.
6. If there is no record in the app, talk to the merchant.

Issuance fees and the 1% and 1.2% general transaction fees are on the [fees page](/en/articles/redotpay-fees). They are not decline fees.
