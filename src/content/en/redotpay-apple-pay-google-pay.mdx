People look up “RedotPay Apple Pay” because a virtual card is more useful if a phone can pay with it. RedotPay’s own page is narrower than a lot of roundups. This is that page, plus the limits it does not close.

Checked on **24 October 2026**.

## What the help center says

[How do I use my RedotPay card for payments?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11027588-how-do-i-use-my-redotpay-card-for-payments) says the virtual card can be linked to **Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal**.

That sentence is the source. It is not a map of which countries’ wallets accept the card. Apple Pay and Google Pay are not offered in every country, and a wallet can refuse a card even when the help center documents the link. If the add fails, retrying from a blog’s screenshots will not change the wallet’s decision.

The [where-to-use article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11027583-where-can-i-use-my-redotpay-card) (4 September 2026) says the card is accepted at 130 million+ merchants on major card networks, online and in person, and that some merchants block prepaid or crypto-linked cards. A successful wallet add is still not a promise that a given shop will approve the payment.

You also need an account RedotPay will verify. US residents cannot be issued a card, so a wallet guide does not apply there. Other blocks are in the [country lists](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries).

## How to add it

RedotPay does not publish a single click-path that we can quote as universal. Use this order:

1. Issue and activate the card. A frozen card will not add.
2. If the RedotPay app shows an add-to-Apple-Wallet or add-to-Google-Wallet button, follow that button.
3. If it does not, open Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and add a card manually with the **number, expiry, and CVV** from the RedotPay app.
4. Finish whatever check the wallet asks for. We are not going to invent whether that check is SMS, email, or an in-app code. It is whichever prompt you see.
5. For Apple Watch, add the card from the Watch app on the iPhone after the iPhone wallet has it. That is Apple’s normal path, not a RedotPay-specific promise.
6. On Android, NFC has to be on, and Google Wallet has to be allowed to pay.

PayPal is a different linking flow inside PayPal, using the virtual card details. It is not Apple Pay.

## Paying in a shop

Hold the phone near the terminal, or use the wallet button online where the site offers Apple Pay or Google Pay. The merchant still runs an authorization. A decline can be the merchant’s rule, an insufficient balance, a currency check, or a frozen card. The app’s transaction line is the record. We do not publish a decline code list RedotPay has not published.

## Pro cashback, if you pay for Pro

On [Getting started with RedotPay Pro](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14686020-getting-started-with-redotpay-pro) (4 September 2026), both Pro plans include **3% back on Apple Pay and Google Pay purchases, up to 18 USD a month**. The cashback is spend-only and expires after 30 days. Pro is optional and non-refundable: **12.90 USD** monthly or **129 USD** annual. The standard card does not include this cashback. The monthly plan’s one-time waiver is the 10 USD virtual fee, not the physical card.

Card-network and FX context for ordinary spending is on the [fees page](/en/articles/redotpay-fees). The 1% and 1.2% rates live on the ATM article, which does not say they cover every wallet purchase. A new card can take up to 5 days to sync with PayPal or Google Pay. If the charge fails, start with the [declined-payment guide](/en/articles/redotpay-card-declined). Named merchants such as Netflix and ChatGPT are in the [merchant guide](/en/articles/redotpay-netflix-chatgpt).
