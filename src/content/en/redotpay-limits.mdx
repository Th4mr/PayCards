“RedotPay limit per day” is answered by a chart image, not by a sentence in the help article. The page [RedotPay Card Limitations & Fees](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339271-redotpay-card-limitations-fees) was updated on **24 July 2026**. The chart file on that page is dated 7 July 2026 in its URL. We read the image on **24 October 2026**. The article text above the image does not repeat the numbers. If RedotPay replaces the image, these figures move with it. The amount in your app still wins for your account.

## Spend caps on the chart

The limits table has no date of its own. The chart page was updated on **24 July 2026**. The line “Effective from 1 December 2025” on the image belongs to the fees table above it, not to these caps. Both value columns show the same spend caps:

| Line on the chart | Figure we read |
|---|---|
| Per transaction | 100,000 USD |
| Daily limit | 1,000,000 USD |
| Deposit fee | Free |
| Balance limit | Unlimited |

The column headers are card-programme labels. The spend numbers under them match, so this page does not invent a difference between them. A header that is hard to read is not a reason to split 100,000 into two products.

[How do I set my RedotPay card limit?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339281-how-do-i-set-my-redotpay-card-limit) (24 July 2026) is the control you have: Card → Limit → a per-transaction limit and a daily spending limit. The checkout-failure article says a payment can fail because of that self-set limit. Setting 50 USD and then trying to pay 60 USD is a decline you created. The company’s chart is the ceiling described above, not a promise that every account is left at 1,000,000 USD.

## ATM caps, separate from the 2% fee

The same image, in the limits table (no date of its own; the chart page was updated on 24 July 2026):

| Line | What the chart shows |
|---|---|
| Withdrawals | 3 a day and 30 a month, in both columns |
| Daily ATM | 23,000 HKD and 3,750 USD |
| Monthly ATM | 75,000 HKD and 50,000 USD |
| Note | For USD cards, in some countries and regions, daily limits are 3,000 USD and monthly limits are 10,000 USD. Actual cash withdrawal amounts apply. |

The **2% then 3%** USD ATM fee, and the **10,000 USD** threshold inside that fee, are on the ATM help page. They are the price of a withdrawal, not this cap. Do not add them together into a new number. Fee steps are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal). Cash needs the physical card, and some addresses never receive one. Timing is in the [delivery guide](/en/articles/redotpay-physical-card-delivery).

## Decline and small-authorization lines on the fees table

These lines sit in the fees table. The chart marks that fees table effective from **1 December 2025**. They are not part of the limits table, which has no date of its own.

| Line | What the chart shows |
|---|---|
| Declined transaction fee | Waived for the first 3 declined transactions per card each month, then 0.5 USD. Examples on the chart: insufficient balance, frozen card, lost card, incorrect card information. The list says it is not exhaustive. |
| Small authorization | Waived for the first 5 transactions per card each month that are 1.00 USD or less, then 0.2 USD. |
| Account maintenance | Waived, including monthly and annual fees. This matches the inactivity article. It is not a waiver of Pro. |

A different page says a **truly declined** transaction is not charged, and a pending hold is usually released in 2 to 5 days. That is the purchase, not this 0.5 USD line. Both are quoted in the [declined-payment guide](/en/articles/redotpay-card-declined).

## Limits this chart does not set

- **Another bank’s card**, if you move money onto RedotPay that way. [Is there a limit on card transfers to RedotPay?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10621403-is-there-a-limit-on-card-transfers-to-redotpay) (23 July 2026) says those limits are usually the bank’s or the card provider’s.
- **Crypto withdrawal minimums.** The on-chain withdraw article says the balance must be above the minimum shown for that withdrawal. It does not print one number for every asset. Steps are in the [withdrawal guide](/en/articles/redotpay-withdraw-bank-paypal).
- **Country blocks.** A high cap does not issue a card where issuance is blocked. See the [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries).
