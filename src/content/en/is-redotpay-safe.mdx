“Is RedotPay legit?” is a fair question, and a blog score is the wrong answer. This page stays inside what RedotPay has published about security, identity, and who is blocked. Figures and rules were checked on **24 October 2026**. PayCards is independent. A referral link is not a safety rating. The [disclosure](/en/disclosure) says how those links work.

## The balance sits in the app

The [services page](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11070044-redotpay-services-and-features) (24 July 2026) describes a wallet inside the app that receives crypto and converts it when you pay. That is a custodial balance: RedotPay holds it. We did not find a help page that gives a seed phrase for that balance. Keep in the app only what you plan to spend.

The same page is not a promise that every deposit arrives. The deposit guide is separate, and a wrong network can lose the funds. Steps are on the [top-up page](/en/articles/how-to-top-up-redotpay).

## Identity checks are mandatory

[Understanding identity verification](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339264-understanding-identity-verification) (26 July 2026) says verification is required. It names Sumsub as the provider, and it lists name, date of birth, and a passport, national ID, or driver’s licence, plus the checks the app asks for. The pages we read do not publish one review time for every country. Do not pay an outside person who offers to speed it up.

## Account security options

[How do I secure my RedotPay account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339300-how-do-i-secure-my-redotpay-account) (26 July 2026) points to Profile, then Security. The options it names are a passkey, email, phone, and Google Authenticator. Turn on the ones the app offers before you deposit.

## Card controls, including freeze

[How do I manage my RedotPay card security settings?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339299-how-do-i-manage-my-redotpay-card-security-settings) (24 July 2026) describes per-purchase and daily limits, toggles for online payments, in-person payments, and ATMs, currency toggles, and a freeze. The same page says some fees on declined payments may still apply while the card is frozen. A freeze is a control, not a refund.

## Transaction screening can hold funds

[What is Know Your Transaction (KYT)?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339287-what-is-know-your-transaction-kyt) (4 September 2026) says RedotPay screens transactions in real time. It may hold, freeze, or decline a transaction, and it may report it. That is a real limit on access to the balance. It is also the kind of control a company publishes when it says it follows sanctions and financial-crime rules. It is not a guarantee your transfer will clear.

## What these pages do not say

The help pages linked above do not publish an insurance amount for the wallet, and they do not publish an audit report. We do not fill that gap with a score, a star rating, or a claim copied from a review site.

The card terms still name a Hong Kong Visa programme and a Singapore Visa programme. The where-to-use article still says 130 million+ merchants on major card networks. Those sentences describe the product. They are not a safety certificate. The [review](/en/articles/redotpay-review) keeps the wording next to the fees.

## A US licence is not US access

On 20 August 2026 RedotPay said it received its first state-level US money transmitter licence, with applications pending in more than 20 other states. The same post says that, as of publication, Red Dot Payment (US), LLC had not engaged in money transmission. [Announcement](https://www.redotpay.com/news/redotpay-sets-groundwork-for-us-expansion-with-first-money-transmitter-license). That is groundwork. It does not change the three US blocks below.

## Japan stopped new signups

[Notice to RedotPay users in Japan](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/17634849), checked on 25 October 2026, says the group has not obtained Japanese crypto-asset exchange registration. It has stopped new registrations in Japan and removed the app from the Japanese App Store and Google Play. Service for existing customers in Japan is to be restricted and eventually ended. The notice says customers outside Japan are not affected. RedotPay says it acquired BTC Box, a registered Japanese exchange, to prepare a compliant service. The notice does not give an opening date.

## HTX addresses are unsupported

[Important update on transfers involving HTX](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/17332256), checked on 25 October 2026, says RedotPay does not currently support deposits or withdrawals to or from HTX-linked addresses. A transfer involving HTX may be held for a compliance check. Do not send it again. Use an address the Deposit or Withdraw screen lists.

## Who cannot open it

Availability is part of “is this usable,” and the United States is a clear no on the pages checked on 24 October 2026:

- **Residents** of the United States are ineligible for a virtual card and a physical card.
- People **located** in the United States cannot access the app.
- **Citizens** of the United States cannot complete KYC.

Those are three different help articles. Do not collapse them into one rule. Other countries are blocked too. Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, and the Gulf states named on the [country page](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) were not on those lists when we checked. India was on the card-issuance list. A missing name is not approval.

## If you still sign up

Use the official invite, enter the referral code while the account is being created, and read what the 5 USD spending credit does not pay. That is the [referral code page](/en/articles/redotpay-referral-code). Then confirm the fee in the app before you pay it. The [fee table](/en/articles/redotpay-fees) has the published amounts and the lines that are missing.

A card that skips identity checks is not this product. See [crypto card no KYC](/en/articles/crypto-card-no-kyc).
