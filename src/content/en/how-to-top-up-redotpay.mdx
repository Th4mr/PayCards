Topping up RedotPay is a crypto transfer into the app, not a card top-up at a shop. The mistake that costs money is the network. This page sticks to the deposit article and the services list. It does not invent a network menu RedotPay has not published.

Checked on **24 October 2026**.

## What you can send

[RedotPay services and features](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11070044-redotpay-services-and-features) (24 July 2026) lists BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, S, SUI, TRX, XRP, USDT, and USDC.

[Supported cryptocurrencies, networks, and minimum deposits](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10693364-supported-cryptocurrencies-networks-minimum-deposits) (21 July 2026) says you can deposit USDC, USDT, BTC, ETH, and more. Networks and minimums are on the in-app Deposit page. They can change. We will not print TRC20, BEP20, ERC20, or Solana as “supported” here. Those names are common on exchanges. They are supported for this deposit only if the RedotPay screen is offering that network for that coin.

USDT and USDC are the usual spending balances because they are meant to stay near one dollar. BTC can move between the deposit and the purchase. That is a reason to choose a stablecoin, not a promise about the peg.

## The rule that matters

The network you select on the exchange must be the network on the RedotPay deposit screen. An address is only an address on the network it was created for. A transfer on a different network is the case the help page does **not** describe as combinable. Treat it as funds you may not get back. Send a small test only after you have matched the network, not instead of matching it.

## Steps

### In RedotPay

1. Open the app and the wallet.
2. Tap **Deposit**.
3. Choose the asset, for example USDT.
4. Choose a network **from the list the app shows**. If a network is missing, it is not an option for this deposit.
5. Copy the address, or use the QR code.
6. Read the **minimum** on that screen. The help page says the deposit must meet the minimum after network fees or it will not be credited.

### On the exchange or wallet you send from

1. Choose Withdraw for the same asset.
2. Paste the RedotPay address.
3. Select the **same network** name. Similar names are not the same network.
4. Read the network fee the sender will deduct.
5. Check that what remains after that fee is still at or above the RedotPay minimum.
6. Confirm with the sender’s own security prompt.

### If the amount is short

The deposit article says a deposit below the minimum is not credited. A later deposit of the **same asset on the same network** can be combined with it and then credited. It does not give a public number for the minimum, and it does not say this rescue works across networks.

### While you wait

The transfer needs network confirmations before the balance updates. The help page we checked does not publish a confirmation count for each network. Timing depends on the network and on congestion. Look at the transaction hash on the sender’s side if the app is still empty, and do not send a second transfer on a different network to “speed it up”.

After the balance arrives, the next question is [how to spend USDT](/en/articles/how-to-spend-usdt).

## After the balance shows

Issuing the virtual card costs **10 USD** from that balance. The 5 USD spending reward cannot pay the fee. Details and sources are on the [fees page](/en/articles/redotpay-fees). Moving money back out — a bank in EUR or GBP, an on-chain withdrawal, PayPal, or P2P — is a different set of pages, collected in the [withdrawal guide](/en/articles/redotpay-withdraw-bank-paypal). If you have not created the account yet, enter the referral code during signup, not after. See the [referral guide](/en/articles/redotpay-referral-code).

## Binance Pay

[How do I deposit crypto to my RedotPay account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339284-how-do-i-deposit-crypto-to-my-redotpay-account) (28 July 2026) is a crypto withdrawal from your own wallet or exchange to the address in the app. It uses USDT-TRC20 as its worked example, and it says that network is for USDT only. It does not mention Binance Pay. The networks article still says the networks and minimums that apply to you are on the Deposit screen.

We checked RedotPay’s help center, including announcements, on 24 October 2026. There is no RedotPay page there stating that Binance Pay top-ups stopped on 3 April 2026. News sites have repeated that date. This guide does not treat it as a RedotPay fact until the company, or Binance, publishes a notice we can link. Use the deposit screen.

Country eligibility is separate from a successful deposit. US residents cannot be issued a card. The [country lists](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) have the sources.
