Topping up RedotPay is a crypto transfer into the app, not a card top-up at a shop. The mistake that costs money is the network. This page quotes the deposit article, the services list, and the published minimums image. The row in the app still wins if it differs.

Checked on **25 October 2026**.

## What you can send

[RedotPay services and features](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11070044-redotpay-services-and-features) (24 July 2026) lists BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, S, SUI, TRX, XRP, USDT, and USDC.

Later announcements add assets that list does not name:

- **RLUSD.** [$RLUSD is now live](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/15302279) (29 May 2026): deposit and spend from 2 June 2026, 4:00 PM (UTC+8). Withdrawal from 9 June 2026, 4:00 PM (UTC+8).
- **BCH and LTC.** [BCH](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/15646549) and [LTC](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/15646560) (both 25 June 2026): deposit from 26 June 2026, 4:00 PM (UTC+8); spend from 30 June 2026, 4:00 PM (UTC+8); withdrawal from 15 July 2026, 4:00 PM (UTC+8).
- **GRAM.** On 27 July 2026 RedotPay said TON is converted to GRAM, and that GRAM can be added and spent. [Post](https://x.com/RedotPay/status/2081674294439129260).

USDT and USDC are the usual spending balances because they are meant to stay near one dollar. BTC can move between the deposit and the purchase. That is a reason to choose a stablecoin, not a promise about the peg.

## The rule that matters

The network you select on the exchange must be the network on the RedotPay deposit screen. An address is only an address on the network it was created for. A transfer on a different network is the case the help page does **not** describe as combinable. Treat it as funds you may not get back. Send a small test only after you have matched the network, not instead of matching it.

## Steps

### In RedotPay

1. Open the app and the wallet.
2. Tap **Deposit**.
3. Choose the asset, for example USDT.
4. Choose a network **from the list the app shows**. If a network is missing, it is not an option for this deposit.
5. Copy the address, or use the QR code.
6. Read the **minimum** on that screen. The help page says the deposit must meet the minimum after network fees or it will not be credited.

### On the exchange or wallet you send from

1. Choose Withdraw for the same asset.
2. Paste the RedotPay address.
3. Select the **same network** name. Similar names are not the same network.
4. Read the network fee the sender will deduct.
5. Check that what remains after that fee is still at or above the RedotPay minimum.
6. Confirm with the sender’s own security prompt.

### If the amount is short

The deposit article says a deposit below the minimum is not credited. A later deposit of the **same asset on the same network** can be combined with it and then credited. The public minimums are the image above. The rescue is not described as working across networks. RLUSD, BCH, LTC, and GRAM are not on that image, so their minimum is the one on the Deposit screen.

### While you wait

The transfer needs network confirmations before the balance updates. The help page we checked does not publish a confirmation count for each network. Timing depends on the network and on congestion. Look at the transaction hash on the sender’s side if the app is still empty, and do not send a second transfer on a different network to “speed it up”.

## After the balance shows

Issuing the virtual card costs **10 USD** from that balance. The 5 USD spending reward cannot pay the fee. Details and sources are on the [fees page](/en/articles/redotpay-fees). Moving money back out — a bank in EUR or GBP, an on-chain withdrawal, PayPal, or P2P — is a different set of pages, collected in the [withdrawal guide](/en/articles/redotpay-withdraw-bank-paypal). If you have not created the account yet, enter the referral code during signup, not after. See the [referral guide](/en/articles/redotpay-referral-code).

## Buy USDT or USDC with a bank card

On **9 November 2026**, RedotPay’s X account ([@RedotPay](https://x.com/RedotPay)) said you can buy USDT or USDC in the app with a debit or credit card, with lower fees than before. The post did not give a fee figure. “Lower” is not a percentage you can copy onto this page.

[How do I buy crypto with a card on RedotPay?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10650778-how-do-i-buy-crypto-with-a-card-on-redotpay) (24 July 2026) is the how-to:

1. Tap **Buy Coins** and choose the crypto you want.
2. Enter the amount and select **Credit/Debit Card**.
3. Review the payment details and tap **Confirm**.
4. Add your card details and tap **Pay**.

The help page does not print a fee. Pay the amount on the review screen, and do not confirm a figure you have not read there. A **RedotPay card cannot be used** to buy crypto. Your bank may ask you to confirm the payment on their side. Crypto usually arrives within **0.5–2 hours**. Some assets are not available in some countries. The page’s example is that **USDT is not available in the UK**.

This is not a crypto deposit. A deposit still has to match the network on the Deposit screen. A card purchase does not replace that rule when you are sending crypto in from an exchange.

A euro, pound, or dollar transfer into a personal funding account is a third path, with its own opening fee rules: [RedotPay IBAN](/en/articles/redotpay-iban).

## Binance Pay

Binance Pay top-ups to RedotPay ended effective **3 April 2026**. That date is in [Binance’s announcement](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/4462aa049244459ab6f9f281199bdc5c). [Cointelegraph’s report](https://cointelegraph.com/news/binance-redotpay-clash-singapore-lawsuit) of the dispute gives the same cutoff.

[How do I deposit crypto to my RedotPay account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339284-how-do-i-deposit-crypto-to-my-redotpay-account) (28 July 2026) is a crypto withdrawal from your own wallet or exchange to the address in the app. It uses USDT-TRC20 as its worked example, and it says that network is for USDT only. It does not mention Binance Pay. Use the deposit address and the network on the Deposit screen.

Country eligibility is separate from a successful deposit. US residents cannot be issued a card. The [country lists](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) have the sources.
