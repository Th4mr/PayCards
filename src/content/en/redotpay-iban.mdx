Searches for “RedotPay IBAN”, “RedotPay IBAN country”, “RedotPay IBAN fees”, and “RedotPay IBAN euro” are asking for a receiving account, not for the card’s ATM. The help center answers some of that in numbers and leaves the country list to the app. Checked on **15 November 2026**.

## It is not a bank account

[What is a personal funding account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990936-what-is-a-personal-funding-account) (23 July 2026) says it is a digital account that works like a traditional bank account and exists only online, so you can receive funds with no branch. The same page says RedotPay is a **fintech service provider and not a bank**. The multi-currency wallet is provided by licensed financial institutions, and RedotPay facilitates your use of it.

[How is it different from a bank account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990938-how-is-the-personal-funding-account-different-from-a-bank-account) (23 July 2026) repeats: no branch, you apply in the app, there is an identity and risk check, RedotPay charges no fee on EUR, GBP, or USD deposits, and you apply for USD, GBP, or EUR deposit details.

[Are my funds safe?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990941-are-my-funds-safe-in-a-personal-funding-account) (23 July 2026) says funds are held with licensed, regulated partners, and it recommends two-factor authentication and biometric login. It does not print a deposit-insurance amount. Do not add one.

## Which countries: the page does not list them

[Which countries and regions can open a personal funding account](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990956-which-countries-and-regions-can-open-a-personal-funding-account) (23 July 2026) is the page people expect to be a country table. The published text is:

> You can open a USD, GBP, or EUR funding account, but availability depends on your country or region and what RedotPay currently supports. To see which accounts are available to you, open the RedotPay app and go to Deposit, then Fiat.

There is no country name on that page. Quoting a list that is not there would be an invention. Open **Deposit**, then **Fiat**. If a currency is missing, that screen is the answer for your account today.

[Which currencies can I open an account in?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990957-which-currencies-can-i-open-an-account-in) (23 July 2026) names three: **USD, GBP, and EUR**. It says more may be added later. It does not name a fourth.

Card block lists are a separate product. A place that can or cannot be issued a card is in the [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries). Those lists do not say who receives an IBAN.

## What you need before you apply

[What do I need to apply?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990943-what-do-i-need-to-apply-for-a-personal-funding-account) was updated on **12 November 2026**:

- Complete identity verification: a valid government ID, which the page names as a passport, national ID, or driver’s licence, plus facial recognition. In some cases a real-time selfie match is required.
- Pass a risk-assessment questionnaire, with **up to 5 attempts**. It covers source of funds, intended use, and occupation.

[What information do I need to open one?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990953-what-information-do-i-need-to-open-a-personal-funding-account) (23 July 2026) asks for first name and last name in **English characters only**, plus country or region, province or state, city, address, and postal code. Incomplete details can delay the application.

[How do I open accounts in more than one currency?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990964-how-do-i-open-accounts-in-more-than-one-currency) is the related article if you want a second currency after the first. Read it in the app’s flow. This page does not invent a click path the article did not need for the fee and IBAN facts below.

## The details you receive, including the IBAN

[What details will I receive after opening an account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990965-what-details-will-i-receive-after-opening-an-account) (23 July 2026):

| Currency | Details the page lists |
|---|---|
| EUR | Account name, IBAN, bank name |
| GBP | Account name, account number, sort code, bank name |
| USD | Account name, account number, SWIFT/BIC code, bank name |

You can view them in the app. Use the exact details shown for the currency you are receiving. A euro transfer uses the IBAN on that screen, not a number copied from a screenshot in a chat.

## Fees

Two pages date the opening fee to **23 July 2026**.

[Is it free to open a personal funding account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11991596-is-it-free-to-open-a-personal-funding-account):

| Situation | Opening fee |
|---|---|
| Balance at least 100 USD, or equivalent | No opening fee for EUR, GBP, or USD |
| EUR or GBP account, balance below that | 6 EUR or 6 GBP |
| USD account, balance below that | 6 USD |

[Is there an account opening fee?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990966-is-there-an-account-opening-fee) says the same threshold in shorter form: at least 100 USD or equivalent means no fee, otherwise a **6 USD or equivalent** fee may apply. Once the account is open, you can withdraw, transfer, spend, or swap regardless of whether the EUR or GBP account is activated. The longer page is the one that splits 6 EUR, 6 GBP, and 6 USD. If the app shows a different figure, pay the screen.

**Deposits.** [Are there any fees to deposit?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11991601-are-there-any-fees-to-deposit-into-my-personal-funding-account) (23 July 2026) says no: RedotPay does not charge to deposit USD, GBP, or EUR. [What’s the deposit fee policy?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990992-what-s-the-deposit-fee-policy) (23 July 2026) repeats that, and adds that if a deposit fails and is returned, the intermediary or receiving bank may deduct a handling fee. Those are the bank’s fees. [What is the refund handling fee?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11991607-what-is-the-refund-handling-fee) (23 July 2026) says that for a small deposit the bank fee can be larger than the amount, so there may be nothing left to return.

**Annual fee.** The free-opening page says the annual maintenance fee works the same for EUR, GBP, and USD. To avoid it, complete at least **1,000 USD or equivalent** in deposits within the first **11 months** after opening. In the 11th month RedotPay notifies you by email, in-app message, or push if you have not met that. **The page does not print the annual fee amount.** Do not guess it. If the notice arrives, the amount in that notice is the one that applies.

Crypto deposit minimums, including 1.00 USDT, are a different table: [how to top up](/en/articles/how-to-top-up-redotpay). Buying USDT with a bank card inside the app is a third path on that same page, and it is not this IBAN.

## How a transfer has to arrive

[What deposit methods are supported?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990987-what-deposit-methods-are-supported) (23 July 2026): bank transfer through your local payment network. The examples are **SEPA for EUR** and **FPS for GBP**. The exact method depends on the currency and is shown when you deposit. The transfer must come from a **personal** bank account. A corporate or business account will not match the verified identity and may fail.

[What are the requirements for a successful deposit?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990976-what-are-the-requirements-for-a-successful-deposit) (23 July 2026) says to use the bank account or payment provider shown in the app for that currency.

[When will my deposit arrive?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990990-when-will-my-deposit-arrive) (23 July 2026):

| Currency | Usual time on that page |
|---|---|
| EUR and GBP | Usually within 1 business day (T+1), from when your bank sends the funds |
| USD | Usually the same day, up to 5 business days in some cases |
| Compliance review | May take 1–7 business days |

Your bank’s speed can change the clock. Track status in the app.

## After the money is there

[How can I use my USD, GBP, or EUR balance?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11990973-how-can-i-use-my-usd-gbp-or-eur-balance-after-depositing) (23 July 2026) says you can swap into other currencies, for example EUR or GBP to USDC or USDT, send international transfers, apply for and spend with the RedotPay card, or withdraw.

[Can I withdraw EUR or GBP to a bank account?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11991671-can-i-withdraw-eur-or-gbp-to-a-bank-account) (30 July 2026) says yes: select that balance and withdraw to a linked bank account in the **same currency**. That page does not print a withdrawal fee. Outbound paths, including the partner-bank warning, are in the [withdrawal guide](/en/articles/redotpay-withdraw-bank-paypal).

[Will I automatically get a card?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11991675-will-i-automatically-get-a-card-with-my-personal-funding-account) (23 July 2026) says no. The card is a separate application, with its own issuance fee: 10 USD virtual and 100 USD physical on the [fees page](/en/articles/redotpay-fees). A funding account does not waive those.

## Before you send a euro

1. Open Deposit, then Fiat. If EUR is not offered, there is no IBAN to copy.
2. Finish the ID check and the questionnaire. Names are in English characters.
3. Read the opening fee against your balance. 100 USD or equivalent is the published free line. Below that, read 6 in the account’s currency.
4. Send from a personal account, on the network the app names, to the IBAN or sort code on the screen.
5. If the transfer bounces, assume a bank may keep a handling fee. Do not send a test so small that the return fee can consume it, unless you accept that outcome.
