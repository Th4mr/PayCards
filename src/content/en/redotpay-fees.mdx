“RedotPay fees” is the search that should be answered with a table, not a slogan. The numbers below are copied from RedotPay’s help center. They were checked on **25 October 2026**. If the app shows a different amount, trust the app. We change this page when we re-check the sources, not when a fee blog elsewhere publishes a figure.

## Four kinds of cost

1. **Once:** issuing the virtual or physical card.
2. **When you spend:** crypto conversion at 1%, and 1.2% when the currency is not the card currency. Both are general transaction fees in section 2, Transactions, of the limitations chart.
3. **Cash:** ATM withdrawals, physical card only.
4. **Outside RedotPay:** the network fee on a deposit, and a fee the ATM operator adds.

## How to read the main lines

### Issuance

The virtual card is **10 USD once**, taken from the balance. The [issuance article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10566200-are-there-any-fees-for-getting-a-card) (27 July 2026) says the 5 USD reward cannot pay it. A separate page says the purchase fee is not credited back to the account. The physical card is **100 USD once**. The in-app control is labeled “Apply for card • 100 USD”, and the [physical-card article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10721338-how-do-i-get-a-physical-card) (30 July 2026) says payment confirms the order and it cannot be cancelled. Those steps name the 100 USD fee and do not list postage. The limitations chart does: standard mail is marked waived, and DHL is calculated at checkout. The day counts are in the [delivery guide](/en/articles/redotpay-physical-card-delivery).

### Monthly fee

The [inactivity article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10566233-are-there-any-fees-if-i-don-t-use-my-redotpay-card-for-a-month) (28 July 2026) says there is no management fee and no monthly or annual fee on the standard card. The 5 USD reward lasts 30 days. Pro is a separate subscription.

### Conversion and foreign currency

Section 2, **Transactions**, of [RedotPay card limitations and fees](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339271-redotpay-card-limitations-fees) (page updated 24 July 2026) lists the 1% and 1.2% rates as general transaction fees, not as ATM-only lines:

- A transaction in the card currency: no extra fee on the [ATM article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10622095-can-i-withdraw-cash-from-an-atm-and-are-there-any-fees) (27 July 2026).
- A transaction in another currency: **1.2%**, in section 2 of the limitations chart.
- Crypto conversion: **1%**, in the same section.

Compare the debit in the app with the merchant’s price. The same fees table on that chart, marked effective from 1 December 2025, also shows **0.2 USD** after the first 5 transactions per card each month of 1.00 USD or less, and **0.5 USD** after the first 3 declined transactions per card each month.

If a foreign terminal offers to convert the amount for you, that is a second layer, on top of whatever RedotPay charges. Decline it when you can and let the card currency do the conversion.

### ATM

Physical card only. **USD card:** 2% up to 10,000 USD in a month, then 3% on the amount above that. **HKD card:** 2%. The ATM operator can add a fee on the machine. Full steps are in the [ATM guide](/en/articles/redotpay-atm-withdrawal).

### Selected merchants

**1.5%, minimum 0.50 USD.** The first three eligible transactions per card each calendar month are waived. The counter resets on the 1st at 00:00 UTC. The list on the [merchant-fee article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339275-which-purchases-have-a-transaction-fee) (3 September 2026) is Gulf Health Counsel, Free Fire, PUBG Mobile, Brawl Stars, eFootball, Facebook Ads, and Discord. **Google Ads is not on the list.**

### Replacement

Virtual replacement is **5 USD the first time, then 10 USD**, with at least 31 days between replacements. Rewards cannot pay it. The PAN stays; CVV and expiry change. [Replacement article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339267-how-to-replace-your-virtual-card) (24 July 2026). The physical replacement fee was not on that page.

### RedotPay Pro

Optional and non-refundable. [Getting started with RedotPay Pro](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14686020-getting-started-with-redotpay-pro) (4 September 2026):

- **Monthly, 12.90 USD:** a one-time waiver of the 10 USD virtual fee, and 3% back on Apple Pay and Google Pay purchases up to 18 USD a month. The cashback is spend-only and expires after 30 days. The monthly plan does **not** waive the physical card.
- **Annual, 129 USD:** a one-time waiver of the 100 USD physical fee, the same cashback cap, and a waiver of RedotPay’s own ATM fees on up to 1,000 USD a month. Operator fees are excluded. Where Earn exists, the annual plan adds 1% APY on the first 1,000,000 equivalent. We are not repeating Earn rules beyond that sentence, because availability depends on the account.

A bank prepaid Visa is a different product. The crypto-card version of that search is [prepaid Visa card online](/en/articles/prepaid-visa-card-online).

## A way to estimate a month

Add three things you can see, and leave out anything the table marks as unpublished:

- Issuance, once, divided by the months you expect to use the card.
- The 1% conversion fee or the 1.2% other-currency fee when that is how you pay. Both are general transaction fees.
- ATM withdrawals times the USD or HKD rate, plus any operator fee on the screen.
- Deposit network fees, which are not RedotPay’s.

Then open the app and compare. A blog total that ignores the in-app quote is the wrong total.
