“RedotPay physical card delivery time” has an official answer in days, and a separate answer about whether your address is allowed to receive a card at all. Checked on **24 October 2026**.

## The ranges RedotPay publishes

[Why hasn’t my physical card been shipped yet?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10566186-why-hasn-t-my-physical-card-been-shipped-yet) (22 July 2026):

1. If the card was **frozen** or **reported lost** during production, production may be paused. Unfreeze it or clear the report. Production resumes when the status is normal again.
2. Check in-app notifications and email for an **address** problem. In the app: Card → **Update now**. Include the full street name and house number.
3. **Production typically takes 10–15 business days.** Public holidays can extend that.

[How long does card delivery take?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10566192-how-long-does-card-delivery-take) (22 July 2026), after the card has shipped:

- **DHL Express:** usually **3–10 days**. Track it on DHL with the tracking number.
- **StandardMail:** usually **5–30 days**, with possible delays from customs or other local factors. Track it on 17track.
- If tracking does not move for a long time, contact support in the RedotPay app.

Add the two stages if you want a planning range: production, then one of those delivery windows. That sum is not a promise. The articles say “typically” and “usually”.

[Can I make payments before my RedotPay physical card arrives?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10566159-can-i-make-payments-before-my-redotpay-physical-card-arrives) (21 July 2026) says yes. As soon as you order the physical card, you can use it online as a virtual card. Online subscriptions do not have to wait for the envelope. ATM cash does. The virtual card does not withdraw.

## What you pay to ship it

[How do I get a physical card?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10721338-how-do-i-get-a-physical-card) (30 July 2026) says the fee is **100 USD**, payment confirms the order, and it cannot be cancelled or refunded. Billing and shipping addresses must be in **English**. The name printed on the card cannot be changed. That article does not price postage.

The chart on [RedotPay Card Limitations & Fees](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/10339271-redotpay-card-limitations-fees) (page updated 24 July 2026) does:

- **Standard Mail: Waived.** Delivery time may vary by destination.
- **DHL Express: varies by country.** The exact shipping fee is calculated at checkout from the address.

“Waived” is what the chart prints next to standard mail. It is not a sentence that every shipment is free. If checkout shows a DHL price, that price is the one you pay. The [fees table](/en/articles/redotpay-fees) keeps the 100 USD issuance line next to this postage line so the two are not mixed.

## When the address is not allowed

Timing articles assume a card can be sent. [Card delivery restrictions](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/14255319-card-delivery-restrictions) (21 July 2026) is a block list. Egypt, Tunisia, and Palestine are on it. So are other places copied in the [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries), including the United States. A name missing from that list is not approval. Do not pay 100 USD to test a blocked address.

Issuance is a different list. A resident who cannot be issued a card never reaches the delivery clock. Morocco is on the issuance list. Read that page before you read the day counts.
