A virtual card for online payments is a card number you can use before any plastic arrives. People search it with “free”, “instant”, and a country. The useful version of the answer is narrower: you load a balance, you pass whatever checks the issuer requires, and the merchant still decides.

RedotPay is one issuer that publishes this product. It is not the only virtual card, and a bank in your country may offer its own. This page describes RedotPay from its help center. It does not review every bank’s virtual Visa.

Checked on **24 October 2026**.

## What you are getting

After the account is verified, RedotPay can issue a **virtual card for 10 USD**, charged once from the balance. The numbers live in the app. You can pay online with them. The [payments article](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11027588-how-do-i-use-my-redotpay-card-for-payments) says a virtual card can be linked to Apple Pay, Google Pay, or PayPal. Whether the wallet in your country accepts that card is not guaranteed. The [Apple Pay guide](/en/articles/redotpay-apple-pay-google-pay) stays on that sentence.

The virtual card does **not** withdraw cash. Cash needs the physical card, which is **100 USD** once, and only if delivery is allowed to your address. See [how to convert USDT to cash](/en/articles/convert-usdt-to-cash).

The [card terms](https://www.redotpay.com/terms/redotpay-card) name Visa for the Hong Kong and Singapore programmes. Newer help pages say major card networks. The app shows the programme you were issued. We do not promise that your virtual card will say Visa.

## What the merchant sees

[Where can I use my RedotPay card?](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/en/articles/11027583-where-can-i-use-my-redotpay-card) (4 September 2026) says the card is accepted at 130 million+ merchants, online where major networks are accepted. The same page says some merchants block prepaid or crypto-linked cards. That second sentence is why a virtual card can fail at a shop that “accepts Visa” in general.

We will not say Netflix, ChatGPT, a game store, or any other named merchant will work. The [merchant guide](/en/articles/redotpay-netflix-chatgpt) quotes the older customer-feedback page and the newer page that does not repeat those names.

## Funding it

The card spends crypto you deposit. The services page (24 July 2026) lists USDT, USDC, BTC, ETH, BNB, SOL, TRX, XRP, SUI, and S. Networks and minimums are on the Deposit screen, not in a fixed table we can print. A below-minimum deposit is not credited. A later deposit of the **same asset on the same network** can be combined and credited. Steps are in [how to top up](/en/articles/how-to-top-up-redotpay).

USDT or USDC is the usual balance for spending because those assets are meant to stay near one dollar. That is not a promise the peg holds. The ATM help page publishes a **1%** crypto-conversion rate and does not say that rate applies to every online purchase. The [fees guide](/en/articles/redotpay-fees) keeps that limit visible.

## Before you pay the 10 USD

US residents cannot be issued a card. Other countries appear on citizenship, location, issuance, or delivery lists. A missing name is not an approval. Read the [country guide](/en/articles/redotpay-supported-countries) first.

Identity verification is required. There is no published path to a RedotPay virtual card without it. See [crypto card no KYC](/en/articles/crypto-card-no-kyc).

If you are comparing this with a bank’s virtual card or another crypto card, use [how to choose a crypto card](/en/articles/best-crypto-card). We do not copy those other fees.
